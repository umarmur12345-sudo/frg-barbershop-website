What We Do

The Full
Experience

From precision skin fades to classic razor shaves — every visit is a ritual. Our barbers are trained in the full spectrum of men's grooming, with a dedicated women's hair area at FRG 3.

Precision Haircuts

Expert cuts tailored to your face shape, hair texture, and style. Classic to contemporary — every time.

Skin Fades & Tape-Ups

Zero-to-skin precision fading with razor-sharp lines. Our signature — what FRG is known for across Brooklyn.

Razor Shaves

Hot towel, straight-razor treatment for the smoothest shave of your life. Old-school craftsmanship, timeless results.

Beard Trims & Shaping

Sculpted beard work with scissors, clippers, and straight razor. Clean edges, full shape, and a crisp finish.

Hair Coloring

Full-spectrum coloring services — subtle highlights to bold transformations. Professional-grade results that last.

Kids Cuts & Line-Ups

Patient, experienced barbers who make every kid feel at ease. Great cuts for every age, every hair type.

✦

Women's Hair Services — Available exclusively at FRG 3 (Bath Beach / Gravesend). Full cuts, coloring, and styling in a dedicated salon area.
