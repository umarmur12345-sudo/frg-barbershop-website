Precision Haircuts

Patient, experienced barbers who make every kid feel at ease. Great cuts for every age, every hair type.

The People Behind It

Meet the Team

The skilled hands and friendly faces behind every cut at FRG.

Frank

Owner

Frank is a seasoned professional with over 30 years of experience in barbering and the proud owner of FRG Barbershop No. 4. Known for his speed and precision, he delivers high-quality cuts efficiently without ever compromising on detail. Highly skilled and versatile, Frank works confidently with all hair types and styles. He is especially great with kids' haircuts, creating a comfortable and friendly experience that puts both parents and children at ease. Sociable and welcoming, Frank has built a strong reputation not just for his talent, but for the positive and relaxed atmosphere he brings to his barbershop every day.

John

Barber

John is an experienced barber with over 5 years in the industry, known for his skill, precision, and dedication to his craft. He handles all types of haircuts and is especially talented at creating custom designs, giving each client a unique and personalized look. John pays close attention to detail, particularly with beard work, ensuring every shape and line is clean, sharp, and well-balanced. Friendly and easy to work with, he creates a comfortable atmosphere where clients feel heard and taken care of every time they sit in his chair.

Ricardo

Barber

Ricardo is a talented barber with 5 years of experience, known for his clean, precise fades and strong attention to detail. He works confidently with both clippers and scissors, making him versatile enough to handle everything from sharp modern cuts to longer textured styles. Ricardo also specializes in custom hair designs, detailed beard grooming, and shaping. To top it off, he offers relaxing facial massages, giving every client not just a fresh cut, but a complete and refreshing grooming experience.

Dario

Barber

Dario is a skilled and dedicated barber with 5 years of hands-on experience in grooming and style. Known for his attention to detail and friendly approach, he offers a full range of services tailored to every client's needs. From clean precise haircuts to stylish kids' cuts, Dario brings patience and professionalism to every appointment. He also specializes in perms and men's hair dye, helping clients achieve bold new looks or subtle transformations. For those looking to elevate their experience, Dario provides VIP haircuts with extra care and precision, along with relaxing facial massages that leave clients feeling refreshed and confident.

Esther

Stylist

Esther is a skilled hairstylist with 27 years of experience, offering professional hair services for the whole family. She specializes in haircuts for women, men, and children, along with men's perms and a variety of styling options. Her expertise also includes colors, braids, and highlights, as well as keratin treatments that leave hair smooth, healthy, and radiant. Esther is dedicated to providing personalized, high-quality service to help every client look and feel their best.

Diana Pulido

PMU Artist & Licensed Lash Technician

Diana is a highly skilled PMU Artist and Licensed Lash Technician with 16 years of experience in the beauty industry. She holds a Tattoo Artist Permit/License issued by the City of New York. Diana offers a wide range of specialized services including micropigmentation of eyebrows, eyes, and lips, Microblading, Ombré Brows, Eyeliner, and Color Lips. She also provides Lash Extensions, Lash Lifting, and Henna Brows. For waxing, Diana specializes in sensitive skin and offers Brazilian waxing (partial or full), as well as facial and body waxing. Additional services include Facial Cleansing, Anti-Stress and Relaxation Body Massage, Professional Makeup for all occasions, and Classic Men's Haircuts.

Sandy

Hairstylist

Sandy is a hairstylist with 10+ years of experience, specializing in balayage, highlights, and custom color for women, including blonding and color transformations. She also offers keratin treatments and modern men's haircuts.

Adrian

Barber

Adrian is a skilled barber with over 20 years of experience, specializing in all hair types and haircut styles. From classic cuts and fades. His extensive experience allows him to work confidently with all textures and lengths, ensuring each client leaves looking sharp and feeling their best. Whether you're after a clean professional cut or a fresh new style, Adrian has the expertise to bring your vision to life.
